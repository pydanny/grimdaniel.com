In this edition of the **Not Dead Yet** newsletter, I talk about the pace of this newsletter. I also mention the first chapter of my next book.

## Slowing down the newsletter

While it's fun to write the "Not Dead Yet" Newsletter every week, it does take time away from my fiction writing. I want to truly focus on crafting new stories. Therefore, the next edition of this newsletter will come out on July 24th.

As I get closer to releasing my next book, I'll return to a more frequent delivery schedule.

## First Chapter of Soldier of the Death God

If you want to read the rough draft of the first chapter of the sequel to "Everyone Dies", you can find it on the new [Soldier of the Death God](/books/soldier-of-the-death-god) page on my website. Based on feedback given last week, I've made updates.
