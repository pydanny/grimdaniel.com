## Iron Meridian: Solarpunk vs Dieselpunk

**Help shape a dieselpunk-versus-solarpunk fantasy before it’s published. Become a beta reader!**

During my vacation I stopped trying to write a sequel to "Everyone Dies" and started on a new book. 

For years, I've had this vision of writing a gritty book about a conflict between two warring states who both conquer new dimensions. One is a dieselpunk empire with a strong religious foundation to control thought. The other is a solarpunk-style empire that uses cultural design to control thought. While one is absolutely better for the environment, both societies have their issues.

While the book isn't finished yet, I'm looking for beta readers. That means people willing to take a look at what I've written so far who are up to sharing their opinion. I need people willing to tell me what I'm doing right and wrong.

[Click here to become a beta reader of Iron Meridian](https://storyoriginapp.com/betacopies/01a04698-ceaa-710b-a40b-dce455514d0d).

I’m looking for feedback on how both empires claim to be the ethical choice. Read it and tell me which one you believed.

## Writerstead launched!

A quick note from behind the scenes: we're (my wife and I) launching [WriterStead](https://writerstead.com/), a website builder and help system made specifically for authors. It’s designed to make the audience-growing side of being a writer a little less tangled. It provides an author-focused website with the tools us writers already use to grow our readership, such as integrations with email lists, StoryOrigin, and BookFunnel. 

My wife built it, I'm a user just like any other writer.

If you’d like to try it out, you can learn more at [WriterStead](https://writerstead.com/).
