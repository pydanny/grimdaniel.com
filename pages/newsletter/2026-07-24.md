In this edition of the **Not Dead Yet** newsletter, I talk about summer and the two stories I'm working on.

## Summer Daze

My daughter is out of school for the summer and finished camp a few weeks ago. Turns out that's a recipe for a life with many distractions. Nevertheless, I'm proud to say I have maintained a writing pace averaging over 500 per day. That's slower than normal, but understandable since I'm apprensive about writing my dark fiction when my daughter is hanging over my shoulder.

Once her school starts up again my writing should return to its normal pace.

## Soldier of the Death God

Set in the same universe as "[Everyone Dies](https://storyoriginapp.com/universalbooklinks/8c487a18-2b3e-11f1-aa1d-dfa5b3422a69)", technically it's not a sequel. That's because everyone died in "Everyone Dies"! Nevertheless, the Lich Queen makes an appearance and it occurs mostly after the events of its predecessor, so I'm calling it "Book 2" of an as-yet-named series.

In any case, I'm making progress on telling a story that mixes first and third person point of view. The premise is that the protagonist serves the Lich Queen as a Dark Captain, and weilds a weapon capable of stealing souls from its victims. When a occurs, he receives some of their memories. Yes, this has shades of [Michael Moorcock's Elric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elric_of_Melnibon%C3%A9).

It has been interesting to write changing point-of-view styles. I tend to write third-person POV, often switching characters between chapters. Writing 1st person for fantasy is new to me, and I can see why some authors like to use it. Expressing thoughts and emotion without falling into the "show not tell" trap feels easier.

## New story: Afterlife

Years ago I read a short story whose name and plot I can't remember, but was set in fantasy afterlife that existed for athiests. Having not been able to ever find it again, I decided to write my own version. I'm about eight chapters in, when I've hit the halfway mark I'll write out the book blurb and run it past you all.
