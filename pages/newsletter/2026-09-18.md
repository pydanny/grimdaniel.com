In this 17th edition of the **Not Dead Yet Newsletter**, I talk about Darkest Autumn, the book that inspired "Everyone Dies", early art for Iron Meridian, talk about Jim Ballard's upcoming new book, and talk about my roleplaying hobby.

## Darkest Autumn 3rd Edition

My novel, "[Everyone Dies](https://grimdaniel.com/books/everyone-dies)," was created in reaction to the first book in a series I co-wrote. Well, my co-author (and wife) Audrey is working on a 3rd edition of that book, titled "Darkest Autumn".  She's also an artist and created a new cover, check it out below.

We'll be releasing the entire series in a variety of places. In the meantime, if you want to get an ARC, go to the official [Ambria series website](https://ambria.writerstead.com/) and sign up the mailing list. When the ARC is ready, we'll contact you.

## Draft art for Iron Meridian

The goal of Iron Meridian is to portray a conflict between two societies, one diesel punk and the other solar punk. This image captures the idea well, with a smoky warship in the front and a solar-powered community in the background. There is an issue with the bow of the ship, yet I think it is nearly there.

## Join me on [startplaying.games](https://startplaying.games/gm/drg)

Writing fiction isn’t the only way I love telling stories. I’ve been running tabletop RPGs for years, and lately I’ve started running games professionally on StartPlaying. There’s something wonderful about sitting down with a group of people and creating a story together. Especially when the players inevitably take it somewhere I never expected. If you enjoy my fiction and you’ve ever wanted to share a table with me, take a look at my [profile on StartPlaying](https://startplaying.games/gm/drg). I’d love to have you in one of my games.
